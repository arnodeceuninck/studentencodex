---
title: Ten green bottles
page: 391
---


**1**\. Ten green bottles hanging on the wall (bis) And if one green
bottle should accidentally fall There\'ll be nine green bottles hanging on the wall.

**2**\.  Nine green bottles hanging on the wall (bis) And if one green bottle
    should accidentally fall There\'ll be eight green bottles hanging on the wall.

...

**10**\. One green bottle hanging on the wall (bis) And if one green bottle
should accidentally fall There\'ll be no green bottles hanging on the wall

> *Engels kinderliedje*
