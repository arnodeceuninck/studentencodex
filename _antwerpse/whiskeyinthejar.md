title: Whiskey in the jar
page: 364-365

**1**\.  As I was a going over  
The Cork and Kerry mountains   
I met with captain Farrell  
And his money he was counting   
I first produced me pistol   
And then I drew me rapier   
Saying: \"Stand and deliver  
For I am your bold receiver\"  

**R**\.  Musha ring dumma do dumma da   
Wack fall the daddy oh  
Wack fall the daddy oh   
There\'s whiskey in the jar  

**2**\.  He counted out his money   
And it made a pretty penny   
I put it in me pocket  
To take home to darling Jenny  
She sighed and swore she loved me   
And never would deceive me   
But the devil take the women  
For they always lie so easy  

**3**\.  I went into me chamber   
All for to take a slumber  
To dream of gold and jewels   
And of course it was no wonder   
Me Jenny took me charges  
And she filled them up with water   
Then sent for captain Farrell  
To get ready for the slaughter  



**4**\.  \'T was early in the morning   
Before I rose to travel  
There came a band of footmen   
And likewise captain Farrell   
I goes to draw me pistol  
For she\'d stole away me rapier   
But I couldn\'t shoot the water   
So a prisoner I was taken  

**5**\.  Well, they put me into jail   
With a judge all a-writing   
For robbing captain Farrell   
On the Kerry mountain   
But they didn\'t take me fists  
Wo I knocked the jailer down   
And I bid a farewell  
To this tight fisted town  

**6**\.  I\'d like to find me brother   
The one that\'s in the army  
I don\'t know where he\'s stationed   
In Cork or in Killarney   
Together we\'d go roving  
O\'r the mountains of Kilkenny   
And I swear he\'d treat me better   
Than me darling sporting Jenny  

**7**\.  Now there\'s some who take delight   
In the carriages a-rolling  
And some take delight  
In the hurling or the bowling   
But I take delight  
In the juice of the barley  
And courting pretty fair maids   
In the morning oh so early
