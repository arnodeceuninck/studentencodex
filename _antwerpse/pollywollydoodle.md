---
title: Polly-wolly-doodle
page: 387
---


1.  Oh, I went down South for to see my Sal Sing polly-wolly-doodle all
    the day! My Sally is a spunky gal

Sing polly-wolly-doodle all the day!

R.  Fare thee well, fare thee well, Fare thee well my fairy fay! For
    I\'m off to Louisiana For to see my Susy Anna

Sing polly-wolly-doodle all the day!

2.  Oh, my Sal, she is a maiden fair Sing polly-wolly-doodle all the
    day! With laughing eyes and curly hair Sing polly-wolly-doodle all
    the day!

3.  Oh, I came to a river and I couldn\'t get across Sing
    polly-wolly-doodle all the day!

And I jumped upon a gator for I thought it was a hoss Sing
polly-wolly-doodle all the day!

4.  Oh, a grasshopper sitting on a railroad track Sing
    polly-wolly-doodle all the day! A-picking his teeth with a carpet
    tack

Sing polly-wolly-doodle all the day!



5.  Behind the barn, down on my knees Sing polly-wolly-doodle all the
    day! I thought I heard a chicken sneeze Sing polly-wolly-doodle all
    the day!

6.  Oh, it sneezed so hard with the whooping cough Sing
    polly-wolly-doodle all the day!

It sneezed its head and its tail right off Sing polly-wolly-doodle all
the day!

> *Amerikaans studentenlied uit 1880*
