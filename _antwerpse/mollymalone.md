page: 363

1.  In Dublin\'s fair city,

Where the girls are so pretty,

I first set my eyes on sweet Molly Malone, As she wheeled her
wheelbarrow, Through streets broad and narrow,

Crying \"Cockles and mussels, alive, alive oh!\"

R.  \"Alive, alive oh! Alive, alive oh!\"

Crying \"Cockles and mussels, alive, alive oh!\"

2.  Now she was a fishmonger, And sure \'t was no wonder,

For so were her mother and father before, And they each wheeled their
barrow, Through streets broad and narrow,

Crying \"Cockles and mussels, alive, alive oh!\"

3.  She died of a fever,

And no one could save her,

And that was the end of sweet Molly Malone. Now her ghost wheels her
barrow, Through streets broad and narrow,

Crying \"Cockles and mussels, alive, alive oh!\"

> *Ierse volkswijze uit de 19de eeuw*
